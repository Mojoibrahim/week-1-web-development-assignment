Guide: How to record your expenses
  1. Enter a brief description in the Expense name field.
  2. Input the numerical value in the Amount field.
  3. Select the best matching Category from the dropdown.
  4. Choose the specific transaction Date.
  5. Click Add Expense to record it to your tracker.
